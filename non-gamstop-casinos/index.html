What GamStop is, precisely

GamStop is Britain's national online self-exclusion scheme, run by the not-for-profit National Online Self-Exclusion Scheme Limited. You register once, free, and every gambling website and app holding a UK Gambling Commission remote licence is required to block your account for the period you choose: six months, one year or five years.

Key facts people get wrong:

  • It cannot be cancelled early. Once the minimum period starts, it runs. This is deliberate and it is the entire point.
  • It does not lapse automatically. At the end of the term you must actively contact GamStop to be reinstated, and there is a further 24-hour cooling-off period after that.
  • It covers online only. Betting shops, casinos and bingo halls run separate schemes — the Multi-Operator Self-Exclusion Scheme for betting shops, SENSE for land-based casinos.
  • It has been mandatory for UKGC licensees since 31 March 2020.
  • It works on your details, not your device — name, date of birth, email, postcode, mobile.

Why some casinos are not on it

Not through evasion, in most cases, but through jurisdiction. GamStop integration is a condition of a UK Gambling Commission licence. An operator licensed by the Curaçao Gaming Control Board or the Anjouan Gaming Authority is not a UKGC licensee, so the condition does not apply to it, and it has no mechanism to check the GamStop register even if it wanted to.

It is worth being clear about the legal position from the operator's side: under section 33 of the Gambling Act 2005, providing facilities for remote gambling to consumers in Great Britain without a UKGC licence is an offence committed by the operator. The Commission pursues this where it can. That is the operator's exposure, not yours — but it is part of an honest picture, and it explains why these sites do not advertise on British television.

Is it legal?

For you, yes. The Gambling Act 2005 places the licensing duty on operators. There is no offence for a British adult who plays at a casino licensed elsewhere, no UK player has ever been prosecuted for it, and your winnings remain entirely tax-free.

For the operator, it is more complicated. Offering remote gambling to GB consumers without a UKGC licence is an offence under section 33 of the Act. Offshore operators generally take the position that they do not target Great Britain specifically. The Commission takes a different view and periodically pursues enforcement, including asking payment providers and ISPs to act.

The honest structural point

Everything the UK Gambling Commission has imposed on licensed operators — stake caps, the credit card ban, affordability checks, no autoplay, minimum spin speeds — exists because those measures demonstrably reduce harm. An offshore casino is a casino with those protections removed. For a player in complete control, that is a set of restrictions lifted. For anyone else, it is a set of guardrails removed from a road they already found difficult. Be honest with yourself about which describes you.

Non-GamStop casino FAQs

What is a non GamStop casino?

An online casino licensed outside Great Britain — typically by the Curaçao Gaming Control Board or the Anjouan Gaming Authority — rather than by the UK Gambling Commission. Because GamStop integration is a condition of a UKGC licence and not of an offshore one, these sites are not connected to the scheme and cannot check the register.

Is it legal to play at a casino not on GamStop?

For you, yes. The Gambling Act 2005 places the licensing requirement on operators, not players, and no UK resident has been prosecuted for playing at an offshore casino. Winnings remain tax-free. The operator's position is different: offering remote gambling to Great Britain without a UKGC licence is an offence under section 33 of the Act, which is why these sites do not advertise on British media.

Can I still gamble if I am on GamStop?

Technically offshore sites remain reachable, because GamStop only binds UK-licensed operators. But you registered for a reason, and these are the sites your registration was designed to keep out. If you are looking for a way around it, that is the clearest possible sign the barrier is working. Install Gamban or BetBlocker, which do cover offshore sites, switch on your bank's gambling block, and call the free National Gambling Helpline on 0808 8020 133.

How do I remove myself from GamStop?

You cannot end an exclusion early — the minimum term you selected must run in full. When it expires, the exclusion does not lift automatically: you must contact GamStop directly to request reinstatement, and a further 24-hour cooling-off period applies before your accounts reopen. This is deliberate and it is the point of the scheme.

Are non GamStop casinos safe?

The games are the same certified software from the same studios, so the gameplay is not the concern. The difference is recourse. You have no IBAS dispute resolution, no Gambling Commission complaint route, and customer fund protection is usually undisclosed. Mitigate that by verifying the licence on the regulator's own register, completing identity checks early, keeping balances low and withdrawing regularly.

Do non GamStop casinos have deposit limits?

Not by law, but all seven sites we test offer deposit limits, loss limits, time-outs and self-exclusion voluntarily in the account settings. No regulator compels them, so nobody will prompt you to use them — which makes setting one before your first deposit rather more important than it is at a UK site, not less.

What happens to my winnings at a non GamStop casino?

They are paid to you exactly as at any casino, and they are completely tax-free in the UK. Check the published withdrawal caps first, though: daily limits at the sites we rank run £2,500 to £5,000 and monthly limits £20,000 to £40,000, and not every operator exempts progressive jackpot wins, which means a large jackpot may be paid in monthly instalments.

Which blocking software works on non GamStop sites?

Gamban and BetBlocker both maintain lists that include offshore operators, unlike GamStop which only binds UK-licensed sites. BetBlocker is free. Combine either with your bank's gambling block — Monzo, Starling, Lloyds, Halifax and NatWest all offer one, several with a 48 to 72 hour cooling-off period before it can be lifted — for a much more effective barrier than any single tool.
