How live dealer casino actually works

The studio

A purpose-built room with broadcast lighting, multiple cameras and a real table. Evolution operates studios in Malta, Latvia, Georgia, Romania, Canada and the Philippines. Dealers work shifts and are recorded continuously.

Optical character recognition

Cameras read the physical cards and the wheel through OCR, converting each real-world event into data the interface can settle against instantly. This is why live results appear on your screen the moment the dealer reveals a card.

The betting timer

Usually 12 to 15 seconds between rounds. Miss it and you sit the round out. Repeat-bet and double-bet buttons let you keep pace without re-placing chips each time.

The stream itself

Adaptive bitrate video, which drops resolution rather than buffering when your connection weakens. That is the correct behaviour — a buffering live table would be unplayable.

Why there is no RNG

Live games use physical equipment, so the "is it rigged" question does not arise in the way it does with slots. The house edge is built into the game rules exactly as in a land-based casino, and the studios are separately licensed and audited.

What happens if you disconnect

The studio's rules apply. In blackjack the dealer plays your hand out to basic strategy; in roulette a placed bet stands and settles normally. You do not lose your stake by disconnecting, but you cannot change a decision.

Every live casino game, and its odds

Classic live blackjack

Seven seats, eight decks, dealer stands on soft 17 at the better tables. 99.28% RTP with basic strategy. Insist on 3:2 blackjack — a 6:5 payout roughly triples the house edge.

Infinite Blackjack

Unlimited players on a single dealer hand, so there is never a wait for a seat. 99.47–99.60% RTP. The Six Card Charlie rule is a genuine bonus; the side bets attached to it are not.

Speed and VIP blackjack

Faster dealing, higher minimums. Identical maths, more hands per hour — which means the house edge is applied to more turnover in the same time.

European live roulette

Single zero, 97.3% RTP. The baseline, and always preferable to American.

French roulette with La Partage

Half your even-money stake returned when zero lands, taking RTP to 98.65% on those bets. The best roulette in any casino and rarely stocked offshore — Kingdom carries it.

Lightning and Quantum Roulette

Random multipliers up to 500x on straight-up numbers, funded by a reduced straight-up payout. RTP falls to about 97.1%. A fair trade for the spectacle, provided you know you are paying for it.

Live baccarat and Speed Baccarat

Banker at 98.94% after commission, Player at 98.76%, Tie at 85.64%. Never bet the Tie. Squeeze variants add drama and change nothing mathematically.

Live casino hold'em and poker variants

Casino Hold'em at 97.84%, Three Card Poker around 96.6%, Ultimate Texas Hold'em around 97.8% with correct play. You play the house, not other players, so there is no bluffing edge to be had.

Dragon Tiger

A two-card baccarat derivative, 96.3% RTP, very fast. Popular in Asian-facing lobbies and increasingly stocked offshore.

Live craps

The pass line at 1.41% plus a true-odds bet behind it — the only bet in a casino with no house edge on the odds portion. Thinly stocked offshore; Seven and Kingdom carry it.

Game shows

Crazy Time at 96.08%, Monopoly Live at 96.23%, Dream Catcher at 96.58%, Mega Wheel around 96.5%. The bonus rounds carry the worst edge in each. Entertainment first.

Side bets, and why to skip them

Perfect Pairs, 21+3, Lucky Ladies, Bet Behind and the rest carry house edges of 4–10% against blackjack's 0.5%. They exist because they are profitable. Declining every side bet is the single easiest way to improve your expected return at a live table.

Live games explained

Live blackjack

The best odds on any live floor. Standard tables seat seven; Infinite Blackjack takes unlimited players on a single dealer hand, which means no waiting for a seat. RTP with correct basic strategy runs 99.28% to 99.60% depending on the ruleset. Look for tables where the dealer stands on soft 17 and blackjack pays 3:2 — a 6:5 payout raises the house edge dramatically and is the single worst rule change you will encounter.

Live roulette

Always choose European (single zero, 97.3% RTP) over American (double zero, 94.74%). French roulette with La Partage is better still at 98.65% on even-money bets, because half your stake returns when zero lands. Lightning Roulette adds random multipliers up to 500x but reduces the straight-up payout, lowering RTP to 97.1% — a fair trade for the entertainment, but know you are paying for it.

Live baccarat

The lowest-effort good-odds game in the building: bet Banker, at 98.94% RTP after the 5% commission. Do not bet the Tie, which carries a 14.4% house edge and exists to catch people.

Game shows

Crazy Time, Monopoly Live, Dream Catcher, Mega Wheel, Sweet Bonanza CandyLand. These now draw more UK players than classic tables and they are genuinely entertaining. They are also the most expensive games on the floor, with RTPs from 92% to 96% and the bonus rounds carrying the worst edge. Play them for the show, not the maths.

Live poker variants

Casino Hold'em, Three Card Poker, Ultimate Texas Hold'em. You play against the house, not other players, so there is no skill edge to be had beyond correct strategy. House edges run 2–3.5%.
