About ChrisChem: The UK Casino and Betting Review Site (September 2026)

ChrisChem is an independent UK guide to online casinos, bonuses and betting sites. We test every site we write about with real money in pounds sterling, we name the people who do it, and we tell you exactly how we are paid.

Written by The ChrisChem Team · Fact-checked by Donna McKean · Updated 4 September 2026 · Do I pay tax?

Why this site exists

There is no shortage of UK casino comparison sites. Almost all of them share three problems: nobody's name is on the page, nobody has deposited their own money into the sites being recommended, and the ranking is a function of commercial deals rather than of testing.

The result is a category where the top-ranked casino is frequently just the one paying most, where "we tested 50 casinos" means somebody read 50 homepages, and where the withdrawal times quoted are lifted from the operator's own marketing.

We built ChrisChem to do the opposite. Two named people. Real accounts, funded with our own money. Withdrawals timed with a stopwatch and screenshotted. A published scoring model with fixed weights. An disclosed commission range of 30% to 50%, one labelled featured slot per offer table, and scored positions that cannot be bought. And criticism of every operator we recommend, including the featured one.

How we are funded

Stated plainly, because you are entitled to know before you read anything else on this site.

When you open an account through a link here, the operator pays us a share of the revenue that account generates. That is standard affiliate marketing and it funds the testing deposits, the writing and the hosting. It costs you nothing and does not change the offer you receive.

Three commitments constrain it:

  1. Commission rates are not identical, and we will not pretend otherwise. Operators pay us between 30% and 50% revenue share depending on the deal. Because the rates differ, our safeguard cannot be "we are paid the same" — it has to be the method. Rankings come from the published weighted scoring model and the order is checkable against it. The evidence it works: Spin Pin pays us 30%, our lowest rate, and outranks four operators paying 45%.
  2. Scored positions cannot be bought. One slot on each offer table is a featured partner placement held by commercial arrangement and labelled as such. That aside, no operator has paid for a position, a badge, a score or a mention, and we will not accept such an arrangement.
  3. We publish criticism of every site we recommend. Read any review here and you will find substantive drawbacks, including in the number one.

What we do not do: sell tips or predictions, run a VIP or rakeback scheme, accept payment to remove criticism, or publish sponsored posts. Full detail on how we make money.

About ChrisChem: your questions

Who runs ChrisChem?

Is ChrisChem a casino or a bookmaker?

No. We do not accept bets, hold player funds, operate games or provide gambling facilities of any kind. We are a publisher of comparison and information content, which is why we are not licensed by the UK Gambling Commission — we carry on no activity requiring a licence. Your account and your money are always with the operator, never with us.

Operators pay us a share of the revenue an account generates when a reader opens one through a link on this site. Rates vary by deal, between 30% and 50% revenue share. No. Scored positions cannot be bought. Every review on this site contains at least one substantive criticism, including of the top-ranked operator. If you find a review here with no drawbacks in it, tell us, because we have failed.

Why do you only list offshore casinos?

Every operator we cover is licensed in Curaçao or Anjouan rather than by the UK Gambling Commission. How often is the content updated?

Operators are re-tested every quarter with a full re-run of the withdrawal script and a fresh RTP audit, and bonus terms are re-verified monthly because they change most often. A licence, ownership or headline-term change triggers an immediate re-test, and a credible pattern of unpaid players removes a site the same week rather than at the next cycle. Every page shows its last verification date at the top.

How do I report an error?

Email editor@chrischem.co.uk with the page URL and what is incorrect, ideally with a source. We aim to respond within two working days and to correct within five. Material corrections are noted on the page itself rather than made silently.

Gambling is entertainment that costs money. It is not an income strategy, the house edge is permanent, and the only reliable way to finish ahead is to stop while you are. Set a deposit limit before you play, never chase a loss, and take regular breaks.
