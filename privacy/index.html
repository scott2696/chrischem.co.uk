Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use chrischem.co.uk, why, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under UK data protection law.

Written by Donna McKean · Fact-checked by Charles Hatfield · Updated 2 September 2026 · Do I pay tax?

1. Who is the controller

ChrisChem is the data controller for personal data processed through chrischem.co.uk. Contact for all data protection matters: privacy@chrischem.co.uk.

We process personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Our approach in short

We collect as little as possible. We do not require registration, we do not run user accounts, we do not sell personal data, and we do not have a login. Most visitors to this site can read everything on it without giving us any personal information at all.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. This site is not intended for under-18s. If you believe we hold data about a child, contact privacy@chrischem.co.uk and we will delete it.

We do not collect special category data (health, religion, political opinions, biometrics and similar), and you should not send it to us.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to anyone, under any circumstances.
  • We do not operate user accounts, logins or profiles.
  • We do not send marketing emails and we do not operate a mailing list.
  • We do not build advertising profiles about individuals.
  • We do not receive your gambling account details, deposits, balances or activity from any operator. Once you leave this site for an operator, we see nothing about what you do there beyond an anonymised commission report.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies. Non-essential cookies — analytics and affiliate attribution — are set only with your consent, obtained through the cookie banner and withdrawable at any time. Full detail, including a table of every cookie, is in our cookie policy.

6. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with processors acting on our instructions, and only where necessary:

  • Hosting and content delivery provider — to serve the website. Processes server logs and IP addresses.
  • Analytics provider — only if you consent to analytics cookies. Configured with IP truncation and no cross-site tracking.
  • Affiliate networks and tracking platforms — only if you consent to marketing cookies, and only a click identifier. These parties are independent controllers for their own purposes; see their own privacy notices.
  • Email provider — to receive and reply to your messages.
  • Professional advisers and authorities — where required by law.

We do not permit any processor to use your data for its own purposes.

7. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or on the International Data Transfer Agreement / UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, with additional safeguards where appropriate. You can ask us for details at privacy@chrischem.co.uk.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS with TLS. We apply access controls to the systems that hold correspondence, keep software patched, and minimise the data we collect in the first place — which is the most effective security measure available to a site like this one. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to us.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used — this policy
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure of your data in certain circumstances
  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances
  • Data portability for data you provided under consent or contract
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent — including for cookies, via the banner or by clearing them in your browser

To exercise any right, email privacy@chrischem.co.uk. We respond within one month, free of charge, and may ask you to verify your identity where the request concerns data we can link to you.

Privacy: common questions

What personal data does ChrisChem collect?

As little as possible. There is no registration, no user account and no login, so most visitors can read everything here without giving us any personal information at all. If you email us we hold your address and message for 24 months. If you accept analytics cookies we receive a truncated IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and referrer, kept for 14 months. Server logs are kept for 30 days for security.

Do you sell my personal data?

No, under any circumstances. We also do not operate user accounts, send marketing email, run a mailing list or build advertising profiles about individuals. We share data only with processors acting on our instructions — our host, our analytics provider if you consented, our email provider, and affiliate networks which receive only a click identifier if you accepted marketing cookies.

Can the casinos see my data through this site?

No. We do not pass your personal information to any operator. If you accept marketing cookies, an affiliate network records that a click came from this site so the referral can be attributed — that is a click identifier, not your identity. Once you open an account with an operator, that operator collects its own data directly from you under its own privacy policy, and it will collect far more than we do because anti-money-laundering law requires it.

How do I request a copy or deletion of my data?

Email privacy@chrischem.co.uk. You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under UK GDPR, plus the right to withdraw consent at any time. We respond within one month, free of charge, and may ask you to verify your identity where the request concerns data we can link to you.

How do I complain about how you handled my data?

Tell us first at privacy@chrischem.co.uk so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. The ICO is the UK supervisory authority and its service is free.

Is my data transferred outside the UK?

Some providers may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens we rely on UK adequacy regulations or on the International Data Transfer Agreement and UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, with additional safeguards where appropriate. You can ask us for details at privacy@chrischem.co.uk.
