What we can help with

If a fact on this site is wrong, tell us. Include the page URL and what is incorrect, and a source if you have one. We respond within two working days and correct within five. Material corrections are noted on the page itself rather than made silently.

Questions about our testing

Ask Daniel anything about a payout time, an RTP figure, a scoring decision or a site we rejected. Our full protocol is published on how we review, and we are happy to explain any individual result.

Questions about bonus terms, law or tax

Ask Priya. She reads the full terms on every offer we publish. Note that we can explain how a term works; we cannot give you personal legal, financial or tax advice, and we will tell you when you need an accountant or a solicitor rather than us.

What we cannot help with

We are not an operator, a regulator or an ombudsman

We cannot recover your money from a casino. We are an independent guide, not a party to your account, and we have no authority over any operator. If a site is refusing to pay you, the escalation route is set out below and on our UK gambling laws page. Do tell us anyway — a pattern of credible reports triggers a re-test and can remove a site from our rankings.

Also outside what we can do:

  • Account access, verification or password problems. Contact the operator's support team; we have no access to any casino's systems.
  • Personal legal, tax or financial advice. We publish general information; your circumstances need a professional.
  • Removing you from GamStop. Only GamStop can do that, and only after your chosen term expires. See how GamStop works.
  • Tips, predictions or systems. We do not sell them and we do not have them. Nobody does.

If a casino will not pay you

The escalation path, in order:

  1. Live chat, then email. Get the specific clause being relied on in writing, and save the transcript.
  2. Formal complaint to the operator through its published procedure, referencing that clause.
  3. The licensing regulator — the Curaçao Gaming Control Board or the Anjouan Gaming Authority, depending on the licence. Expect weeks rather than days.
  4. A mediation service — AskGamblers, Casino Guru or ThePOGG. In practice these resolve more offshore disputes than regulators do.
  5. Tell us. We cannot adjudicate, but we log every report and a pattern removes a site from this page.

Note that IBAS, the Gambling Commission and the Financial Ombudsman are not available for operators without a UK Gambling Commission licence — which is every operator we list, and one of the reasons we say so on every page.

No. We are an independent publisher, not a party to your account, and we have no authority over any operator. The escalation path is set out above: get the specific clause in writing from live chat, raise a formal complaint through the operator's published procedure, then complain to the licensing regulator, and use a mediation service such as AskGamblers, Casino Guru or ThePOGG. Who do I email about a factual error?

editor@chrischem.co.uk, with the page URL and what is incorrect. A source helps but is not required. We aim to respond within two working days and to correct within five, and material corrections are noted on the page rather than made silently.

No. We publish general information and testing data, not predictions, systems or tips, and we do not sell any. We also cannot give personal legal, financial or tax advice — where a question needs a solicitor or an accountant, we will tell you that rather than guess.

I need help with my gambling. Can you help?

We are not qualified to and they are, free of charge. Call the National Gambling Helpline on 0808 8020 133, free and confidential, 24 hours a day, or use the live chat at gamcare.org.uk. Are you a gambling operator?

No. We do not accept bets, hold customer funds or operate any game, which is why we hold no UK Gambling Commission licence — we carry on no licensable activity. If you have an account problem, a verification issue or a payment query, the operator's own support team is the only party that can help.
