Our editorial standards

Every page is authored and fact-checked

No page on this site is published without a named author and a named fact-checker, both shown at the top of the page with the date of last verification. If you cannot tell who wrote a casino guide, you cannot assess whether they know anything — and most of this industry relies on that.

We test with our own money

Every score derives from an account we opened and funded ourselves. We do not accept operator-provided test accounts, because a test account does not experience a real withdrawal or a real KYC review. The full protocol is on how we review.

We publish what is wrong as well as what is right

Every review on this site contains at least one substantive criticism. A casino guide that finds nothing to criticise in seven operators has not tested them.

Commercial arrangements are disclosed and constrained

Operators pay us a revenue share when a reader opens an account through our links, at an identical rate across every brand we list. Position cannot be bought. Full detail on how we make money and on our about us page.

We correct errors and say so

Email editor@chrischem.co.uk with a page URL and what is wrong. We respond within two working days, correct within five, and note material corrections on the page.

We do not sell tips

No predictions, no tipping service, no VIP scheme, no rakeback. If a gambling site is selling you certainty, that is the product being sold — and it does not exist.

Contact us directly

Both authors can be reached individually at the addresses above, or through the general editorial address on our contact us page. We answer readers, and we answer operators who think we have got something wrong — though in the latter case the remedy is a re-test rather than a deletion.
