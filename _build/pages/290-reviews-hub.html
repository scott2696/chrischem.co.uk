How to read a casino review — ours or anyone else's

Find the tested claims

Anything phrased as "fast payouts" without a number is marketing. A review worth reading states what was measured, when, and by whom.

Look for the criticism

A review of a commercial partner containing no drawbacks has not been done. If you cannot find a criticism in the first screen, you are reading an advert.

Check whether the reviewer deposited

"We tested 50 casinos" frequently means somebody read 50 homepages. Deposit, verification and withdrawal are the three things that cannot be assessed from the outside.

Check the verification date

Bonus terms change monthly. A review dated eighteen months ago is describing a different offer.

Check the commercial disclosure

Not whether there is one — there always is — but whether it says how the arrangement is structured and whether position can be bought.

Check for a scoring model

A score with no published weights is a number somebody chose. Ours is on the methodology page with a worked example.
