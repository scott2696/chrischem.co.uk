What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by a website. Cookies let a site remember things between pages and visits — your preferences, for example, or the fact that you arrived from a particular link. Similar technologies including local storage and pixels work comparably, and this policy covers those too.

Our approach

We use as few cookies as we can. Strictly necessary cookies are set automatically because the site cannot function without them; UK law permits this without consent. Analytics and marketing cookies are set only if you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can change your mind at any time.

We do not use advertising networks, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not permit third parties to track you across unrelated websites from this one.

Managing your consent

You can withdraw or change consent at any time by:

  • Reopening the cookie preferences banner and changing your selection
  • Clearing cookies for chrischem.co.uk in your browser, which resets your choice and re-presents the banner
  • Blocking cookies entirely in your browser settings

Rejecting analytics and affiliate cookies does not restrict any content on this site. Every page remains fully readable. The only practical effect is that we lose visibility of which pages are useful, and we may not be credited for a referral.

All others require your prior, informed, freely given consent, which we obtain through the cookie banner and which you may withdraw at any time.

Cookies: common questions

What cookies does this site use?

Four categories, and only two are set without asking. Strictly necessary cookies handle security and load balancing, and a consent preference cookie remembers your choice for twelve months — UK law permits both without consent. Analytics cookies, kept up to 14 months, and affiliate attribution cookies, kept up to 30 days, are set only if you accept them. We use no advertising, retargeting, social media or profiling cookies.

Can I use the site if I reject cookies?

Yes, completely. Rejecting analytics and affiliate cookies restricts no content whatsoever — every page remains fully readable and every feature works. The only practical effect is that we lose visibility of which pages are useful, and we may not be credited for a referral.

How do I change my cookie choice later?

Reopen the cookie preferences banner and change your selection, or clear cookies for chrischem.co.uk in your browser, which resets the choice and re-presents the banner. You can also block cookies entirely in your browser settings — the table above shows where those controls sit in Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox and Android.

Do you honour Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track?

We honour the Global Privacy Control signal where your browser sends one, treating it as a rejection of optional cookies. Browser "Do Not Track" headers are not a recognised standard and are not relied upon, so use the cookie banner or GPC to record a preference.

What is an affiliate cookie and what does it record?

It records that you clicked through to an operator from this site, so the operator can attribute the referral and pay us. It stores a click identifier, not your identity, and lasts up to 30 days. It is set only with your consent, and declining it costs you nothing — the offer you receive from the operator is identical either way.

What law covers cookies in the UK?

The Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 together with the UK GDPR. Strictly necessary cookies are permitted without consent; all others require your prior, informed and freely given consent, which you may withdraw at any time. Questions go to privacy@chrischem.co.uk.
