Live games explained

Live blackjack

The best odds on any live floor. Standard tables seat seven; Infinite Blackjack takes unlimited players on a single dealer hand, which means no waiting for a seat. RTP with correct basic strategy runs 99.28% to 99.60% depending on the ruleset. Look for tables where the dealer stands on soft 17 and blackjack pays 3:2 — a 6:5 payout raises the house edge dramatically and is the single worst rule change you will encounter.

Live roulette

Always choose European (single zero, 97.3% RTP) over American (double zero, 94.74%). French roulette with La Partage is better still at 98.65% on even-money bets, because half your stake returns when zero lands. Lightning Roulette adds random multipliers up to 500x but reduces the straight-up payout, lowering RTP to 97.1% — a fair trade for the entertainment, but know you are paying for it.

Live baccarat

The lowest-effort good-odds game in the building: bet Banker, at 98.94% RTP after the 5% commission. Do not bet the Tie, which carries a 14.4% house edge and exists to catch people.

Game shows

Crazy Time, Monopoly Live, Dream Catcher, Mega Wheel, Sweet Bonanza CandyLand. These now draw more UK players than classic tables and they are genuinely entertaining. They are also the most expensive games on the floor, with RTPs from 92% to 96% and the bonus rounds carrying the worst edge. Play them for the show, not the maths.

Live poker variants

Casino Hold'em, Three Card Poker, Ultimate Texas Hold'em. You play against the house, not other players, so there is no skill edge to be had beyond correct strategy. House edges run 2–3.5%.

Etiquette and how the tables actually work

  • There is a betting timer, usually 12 to 15 seconds. Miss it and you sit the round out. Pre-place bets using the repeat-bet button if you want to keep pace.
  • The dealer can see the chat and will respond by name. They cannot see you. Be civil — dealers are working a shift, and abusive chat gets you removed from the table by the studio, not the casino.
  • Side bets are traps. Perfect Pairs, 21+3, Lucky Ladies and their equivalents carry house edges of 4–10% against blackjack's 0.5%. They exist because they are profitable.
  • Seat availability is real at classic tables. Infinite Blackjack and roulette have no seat limit; standard blackjack seats seven and fills up on Saturday nights.
  • You can watch without betting at most tables. Useful for judging whether a table's pace suits you before committing.
