How live dealer casino actually works

The studio

A purpose-built room with broadcast lighting, multiple cameras and a real table. Evolution operates studios in Malta, Latvia, Georgia, Romania, Canada and the Philippines. Dealers work shifts and are recorded continuously.

Optical character recognition

Cameras read the physical cards and the wheel through OCR, converting each real-world event into data the interface can settle against instantly. This is why live results appear on your screen the moment the dealer reveals a card.

The betting timer

Usually 12 to 15 seconds between rounds. Miss it and you sit the round out. Repeat-bet and double-bet buttons let you keep pace without re-placing chips each time.

The stream itself

Adaptive bitrate video, which drops resolution rather than buffering when your connection weakens. That is the correct behaviour — a buffering live table would be unplayable.

Why there is no RNG

Live games use physical equipment, so the "is it rigged" question does not arise in the way it does with slots. The house edge is built into the game rules exactly as in a land-based casino, and the studios are separately licensed and audited.

What happens if you disconnect

The studio's rules apply. In blackjack the dealer plays your hand out to basic strategy; in roulette a placed bet stands and settles normally. You do not lose your stake by disconnecting, but you cannot change a decision.
