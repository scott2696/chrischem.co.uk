What GamStop is, precisely

GamStop is Britain's national online self-exclusion scheme, run by the not-for-profit National Online Self-Exclusion Scheme Limited. You register once, free, and every gambling website and app holding a UK Gambling Commission remote licence is required to block your account for the period you choose: six months, one year or five years.

Key facts people get wrong:

  • It cannot be cancelled early. Once the minimum period starts, it runs. This is deliberate and it is the entire point.
  • It does not lapse automatically. At the end of the term you must actively contact GamStop to be reinstated, and there is a further 24-hour cooling-off period after that.
  • It covers online only. Betting shops, casinos and bingo halls run separate schemes — the Multi-Operator Self-Exclusion Scheme for betting shops, SENSE for land-based casinos.
  • It has been mandatory for UKGC licensees since 31 March 2020.
  • It works on your details, not your device — name, date of birth, email, postcode, mobile.

Why some casinos are not on it

Not through evasion, in most cases, but through jurisdiction. GamStop integration is a condition of a UK Gambling Commission licence. An operator licensed by the Curaçao Gaming Control Board or the Anjouan Gaming Authority is not a UKGC licensee, so the condition does not apply to it, and it has no mechanism to check the GamStop register even if it wanted to.

It is worth being clear about the legal position from the operator's side: under section 33 of the Gambling Act 2005, providing facilities for remote gambling to consumers in Great Britain without a UKGC licence is an offence committed by the operator. The Commission pursues this where it can. That is the operator's exposure, not yours — but it is part of an honest picture, and it explains why these sites do not advertise on British television.

Is it legal?

For you, yes. The Gambling Act 2005 places the licensing duty on operators. There is no offence for a British adult who plays at a casino licensed elsewhere, no UK player has ever been prosecuted for it, and your winnings remain entirely tax-free.

For the operator, it is more complicated. Offering remote gambling to GB consumers without a UKGC licence is an offence under section 33 of the Act. Offshore operators generally take the position that they do not target Great Britain specifically. The Commission takes a different view and periodically pursues enforcement, including asking payment providers and ISPs to act.

The practical consequence for you: a site could become unavailable at short notice, which is another reason to withdraw regularly rather than let a balance accumulate. Full legal background on UK gambling laws.

Bonuses at non-GamStop casinos, type by type

Every offshore promotion falls into one of six shapes. The size differs from a UK site; since January 2026 the terms differ in the other direction.

Welcome and match bonuses

The casino matches a percentage of your deposit, often laddered across three or four deposits. Headlines of 600% or 1000% describe the total across the whole ladder, not the first deposit. Judge it on the turnover required, not the percentage.

No-deposit bonuses

Bonus cash or free spins for registering, with no payment. Increasingly scarce offshore because they attract abuse at scale. Where offered, expect 40x–60x wagering and a £50–£100 cashout cap, giving a realistic expected value of £4–£8. See our no deposit casinos page for the full arithmetic.

Free spins

Spins on one nominated slot at a fixed stake, usually 10p or 20p. Multiply spins by stake to get the real value — 200 spins at 10p and 100 spins at 20p are both £20 of play. Check whether they are released in one expiring block or drip-fed daily; drip-fed is materially better and much rarer.

Reload bonuses

A smaller match on later deposits, often on a fixed weekday. Kingdom's Monday, Wednesday and Friday calendar is the most developed here and is worth more to a weekly player than the welcome hook.

Cashback

A percentage of net losses returned. Underrated, because it pays on every session rather than once. Rivo's 25% VIP cashback is the largest we list. Check whether it arrives as cash or as bonus funds with wagering — that detail changes its value threefold.

VIP and loyalty schemes

How KYC differs at non-GamStop casinos

This is the practical difference players notice most, and it cuts both ways.

UKGC sites verify before you play

A Gambling Commission licensee must confirm your age and identity before you can deposit or gamble. It is friction at the front door, and it means your first withdrawal is usually clean.

Offshore sites verify at withdrawal

You can typically register and play within two minutes, with checks deferred until you ask for money. Lighter at signup, considerably heavier at cashout — roughly two-thirds of the delayed-withdrawal complaints we investigate are simply KYC being attempted for the first time on a winning balance.

What you will be asked for

Photo ID (passport or UK photocard licence), proof of address dated within three months (bank statement, council tax or utility bill — not a mobile phone bill), a payment method confirmation, and for larger sums a source-of-funds document. Photograph everything flat, on a dark surface, in daylight; a third of rejections are glare or a cropped corner.

The fix

Verify on day one, while you have nothing at stake. It converts a typical first withdrawal from three days to a few hours and removes the single most common cause of a dispute.

What changed in the UKGC rules in 2025 and 2026

Three reforms have landed, and the most recent one flatly contradicts the argument most non-GamStop pages are still making. If a comparison page has not been updated for 19 January 2026, its bonus advice is stale.

Bonus wagering capped at 10x, bonus funds only

The Gambling Commission announced on 26 March 2025 an amendment to Social Responsibility Code Provision 5.1.1. From 19 January 2026, a UKGC-licensed operator may apply a wagering requirement of no more than 10 times the bonus, applied to bonus funds only. The Commission's own example was a £10 bonus at 50x requiring £500 of play.

Read this before you believe any "bigger bonuses" claim

On wagering terms, UK-licensed casinos are now better than the offshore market, not worse. A UKGC site is legally capped at 10x on bonus funds. The sites on this page run 25x to 35x, and some apply it to deposit plus bonus. Smash's 10x, which we call the best terms on this site, merely equals the UK legal maximum. Offshore still wins on bonus size, catalogue and RTP builds. It now loses on wagering, and we have corrected every page here to say so.

Mixed-product incentives banned

From the same date, a UKGC operator cannot require you to gamble on two different products to unlock one reward — no "bet £10 on football, get 50 casino spins". Offshore operators still bundle casino and sportsbook offers, so check which product each offer's turnover must be completed on.

Online slot stake limits

£5 per spin for over-25s from 9 April 2025, £2 for 18–24s from 21 May 2025. UKGC only.

The statutory levy

From 6 April 2025, licensed operators pay up to 1.1% of gross gambling yield into a ringfenced fund for research, prevention and treatment. It raised roughly £120 million in year one, split 50% to NHS-led treatment, 30% to prevention and 20% to UK Research and Innovation. Offshore operators contribute nothing to it.

What has not changed

The credit card ban, mandatory GamStop integration, the 2.5-second spin speed floor and the autoplay ban all still apply to UKGC sites only. Your winnings remain tax-free either way, because that follows you rather than the operator's licence.

The honest structural point

Everything the UK Gambling Commission has imposed on licensed operators — stake caps, the credit card ban, affordability checks, no autoplay, minimum spin speeds — exists because those measures demonstrably reduce harm. An offshore casino is a casino with those protections removed. For a player in complete control, that is a set of restrictions lifted. For anyone else, it is a set of guardrails removed from a road they already found difficult. Be honest with yourself about which describes you.
