## 5. SERP feature targeting

| Feature | How we take it |
|---|---|
| **Featured snippet (paragraph)** | `.cal--info` "The short answer" block opens every page with a 40–60 word direct answer that leads with the entity and the verdict. |
| **Featured snippet (table)** | One clean comparison table per money page, ≤8 columns, `<caption>` stating the date, `<th scope>` on both axes. |

| **Sitelinks** | Consistent nav + footer, clean single-word-ish URLs, no `.html`. |
| **Knowledge panel support** | `Organization` + `WebSite` graph on every page, two `Person` entities with `knowsAbout`. |

## 6. Meta title formula

`{Primary keyword} {qualifier} | {differentiator}` — all ≤60 characters, all
distinct, all avoiding the same modifier twice:

- Tested · Ranked · Compared · Explained · Vetted · Guide · Reviewed
- Never repeat the trailing phrase across two pages.

Descriptions are 115–158 characters, lead with the primary keyword, and end on
a proof point (a number we measured) rather than a call to action.
