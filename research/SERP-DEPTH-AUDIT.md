## 6. Material factual update we must reflect (verified, primary source)

The UK bonus market changed on **19 January 2026** and most affiliate pages have
not integrated it. From the Gambling Commission's own announcement of
**26 March 2025**, amending **SR Code Provision 5.1.1 (Rewards and bonuses)**:

1. **Wagering requirements capped at 10x** the bonus. The Commission's own
   example: "a £10 bonus with a 50 times wagering requirement requires the
   consumer to play through £500."
2. **Wagering applies to bonus funds only** — deposit+bonus wagering is gone at
   UKGC sites.
3. **Mixed-product incentives banned** — an operator can no longer require you to
   bet on sport to unlock casino spins, or vice versa.

Other in-force reforms with exact dates:

| Measure | In force | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Online slot stake limit, 25+ | **9 April 2025** | £5 per spin |
| Online slot stake limit, 18–24 | **21 May 2025** | £2 per spin |
| Statutory levy | **6 April 2025** | Up to 1.1% of GGY; ~£100m target, ~£120m raised in year one; 50% NHS treatment / 30% prevention / 20% UKRI research |
| Bonus rules (SR 5.1.1) | **19 January 2026** | 10x cap, bonus-only wagering, mixed-product ban |

**This inverts a claim we were making.** Our pages framed offshore bonuses as
better value than UKGC ones. Post-19-January-2026 that is wrong: a UKGC site is
capped at 10x on bonus funds only, while the offshore sites we list run 25x–35x,
and several apply it to deposit + bonus. Smash's 10x is no longer "the best in
the market" — it is merely *equal to the UK legal maximum*. Every page that
compares bonus terms must be corrected, and the correction is itself a
differentiator because competitors have not made it.

**Two regressions were caught during this pass and are now guarded in `build.py`:**

1. **FAQ schema had silently dropped to zero.** The template port changed FAQ
   markup from `<details class="faq">` to `<div class="faq"><details>`, and
   `extract_faq()` still matched the old form. Every page rendered its FAQs
   visually while emitting no `FAQPage` schema at all — 300 questions, no rich
   result eligibility. `main()` now asserts that the number of `<summary>`
   elements equals the number of `Question` entries in the schema, per page.
2. **Unbalanced `<div>` from a tag-swap** (caught earlier, guard retained).

**One factual correction was applied sitewide.** Every claim that offshore
bonuses beat UK ones *on terms* has been rewritten, because the 19 January 2026
cap makes it false. Affected: the homepage comparison table and wagering
section, the non-GamStop comparison table, the bonuses page answer box and CTA,
and the Smash, Gambiva and Aphrodite reviews.

## 9. FAQPage schema: now on all 35 pages, with a caveat worth knowing

All 35 pages now emit `FAQPage` structured data — **357 questions**, with the
markup count asserted equal to the schema count on every build. The nine pages
that previously had none (about, authors, contact, terms, privacy, cookies,
how-we-review, casino-reviews, responsible-gambling) were given genuine,
visible FAQ sections rather than schema alone. Injecting `FAQPage` without
matching on-page content is a structured-data violation and risks a manual
action, so it was never an option.

**However: FAQ rich results no longer appear in Google.** Google restricted them
to authoritative government and health sites in August 2023, and multiple SEO
industry sources report full deprecation from **7 May 2026**, with the search
appearance, Rich Results Test support and Search Console reporting withdrawn
across June–August 2026. We could not confirm the 2026 dates from Google's own
blog directly, so treat them as well-sourced industry reporting rather than
primary.

The markup is still worth having:

- It remains valid Schema.org and causes no harm.
- It is still crawled and used by **Bing**, and by AI answer engines and
  retrieval crawlers (PerplexityBot and similar), which is an increasingly
  material referral channel for this category.
- It supports entity and topic understanding independently of rich results.
- The **visible FAQ content** is the part that actually earns traffic: those
  357 questions target long-tail and question-shaped queries directly in the
  page body, which is unaffected by the rich-result change.

**Do not expect FAQ accordions in Google SERPs.** Expect the questions to earn
their keep as body content and as AI-answer source material.
