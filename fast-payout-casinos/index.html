The number that matters is the pending window

Almost every casino holds a withdrawal in a "pending" or "processing" state before it is sent. That window is entirely the operator's choice and it is where the difference between a two-hour payout and a three-day one lives. Kingdom's pending window was consistently under 90 minutes. Two sites I rejected held withdrawals for 72 hours as standard, described in their terms as a "reversal period" — a deliberate design that invites you to cancel the withdrawal and keep playing. If a site offers a "cancel withdrawal" button prominently, that is what it is for.

Why your withdrawal is pending

This is the most-searched question in the category and it deserves a direct answer. In order of how often it is the actual cause:

  1. Identity verification is incomplete. Roughly two-thirds of delayed withdrawals. The casino will not release funds until KYC passes, and it usually only starts KYC when you first ask for money.
  2. A bonus is still active with wagering outstanding. Your withdrawable balance and your total balance are different numbers. Check the cashier for "withdrawable" specifically.
  3. The operator's standard pending window. 0 to 72 hours depending on the site. Disclosed in the terms, usually under "Withdrawal Policy".
  4. You exceeded a daily or weekly cap. The excess is queued to the next period rather than refused, so it looks like a delay.
  5. Withdrawal method mismatch. Most operators require you to withdraw to the method you deposited with, up to the deposited amount, for anti-money-laundering reasons. Depositing by paysafecard and requesting a card payout will stall.
  6. A manual review on a large amount. Above roughly £2,000 many offshore sites add a compliance check. This is normal and usually adds 24 to 48 hours.
  7. Weekend or bank holiday. Applies to cards and bank transfers only.

If none of those applies and you are past 72 hours, escalate in writing through live chat and ask which specific clause is being relied on. The complaints escalation path is set out on our homepage.

Five ways to get paid faster

1. Verify on day one

Upload photo ID and a proof of address dated within three months the moment you register. This is the single biggest lever, and it converts a typical first withdrawal from three days to a few hours. Passport or driving licence, plus a bank statement or utility bill — a mobile phone bill is rejected by most operators.

2. Use crypto or open banking

A sixteen-fold speed difference against debit cards. USDT specifically, if you want the speed without the price volatility.

3. Do not take a bonus if you plan to withdraw quickly

An active bonus locks the balance until wagering completes. If your goal is a fast in-and-out, decline the welcome offer. Your money then has no strings at all.

4. Withdraw to your deposit method, and match the amount

Anti-money-laundering rules at almost every operator require funds to return the way they came, at least up to the amount deposited. Planning your deposit method around your intended withdrawal method removes an entire class of delay.

5. Request before Friday afternoon

For cards and bank transfers only. Crypto and open banking do not care what day it is.

Never cancel a pending withdrawal

The "cancel withdrawal" button exists to return funds to your playable balance. Casinos with long pending windows know exactly what they are doing when they place it prominently, and the industry term for the money recovered this way is "reverse withdrawal revenue". Once you request a payout, leave it alone. If you struggle with this, choose a site with a short pending window — or use the tools on our responsible gambling page.

Is "instant withdrawal" real?

Mostly no, and the phrase is doing a lot of work in casino marketing. Here is what it actually means in practice:

  • "Instant" usually means the operator's approval step is automated rather than manual. That removes the pending window but not the payment rail's own settlement time.
  • The genuinely near-instant case is crypto on a verified account with no bonus active. My 11-minute Kingdom payout was exactly this. Most of those 11 minutes were blockchain confirmations, not the casino.
  • "Instant withdrawal, no verification" does not exist at any legitimate operator. Every licensed site, offshore included, must verify identity before releasing funds. A site promising otherwise is either lying or is not running AML controls, and neither is a site you want holding your money.

Payment methods to avoid if speed matters

  • paysafecard — deposit only. You will need a second verified method to get paid, and setting that up after you win adds days.
  • Bank transfer — the slowest rail, with a median of three working days and a worst case of nine.
  • Credit card — cannot be used for withdrawals at all, and should not be used for deposits either. Your issuer treats it as a cash advance with interest from day one.
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay — deposit-only wrappers around your debit card. Fine going in, useless coming out.

Withdrawal limits and caps

This is the clause that turns a big win into a small annuity, and it is always in the terms rather than the marketing.

Read the "jackpot exception" column carefully. A £20,000 monthly cap with no jackpot exception means a £200,000 progressive win is paid over ten months. That is lawful, disclosed, and exactly the kind of clause that makes headlines when someone discovers it after the fact. Check it before you play jackpot slots anywhere.

Verification: what they ask for and why

Every operator, offshore included, must confirm you are who you say you are before releasing money. It is anti-money-laundering law, not obstruction. What you will need:

  • Photo ID — passport or UK photocard driving licence. Full page, all four corners visible, no glare.
  • Proof of address — bank statement, council tax bill or utility bill dated within three months, showing your name and address. Mobile phone bills are usually rejected.
  • Proof of payment method — a card photo with the middle digits obscured, or an e-wallet account screenshot.
  • Source of funds — only for large amounts or unusual patterns. Payslips or bank statements. Being asked this is normal above roughly £5,000 and is not an accusation.

Photograph documents flat on a dark surface in daylight. Roughly a third of rejected uploads fail on glare or a cropped corner alone, and each rejection costs you another 24 hours.

How long should a casino withdrawal take?

On a verified account with no bonus active: crypto three hours median, open banking eight hours, e-wallets nine hours, debit cards two working days and bank transfer three working days. Anything beyond 72 hours without an explanation is worth escalating in writing.

Why is my casino withdrawal still pending?

Most often because identity verification is incomplete — that accounts for roughly two-thirds of delayed withdrawals. Other common causes are an active bonus with wagering outstanding, the operator's own pending window of up to 72 hours, exceeding a daily or weekly cap, requesting a payout to a method you did not deposit with, or a manual compliance review on a large amount.

Can I withdraw casino winnings without verification?

No. Every licensed operator, offshore included, must verify identity before releasing funds under anti-money-laundering rules. Any site advertising "instant withdrawal, no verification" is either misdescribing an automated approval step or is not running proper controls — neither is a site you want holding your money.

Do fast payout casinos charge a fee?

Six of the seven sites we rank charge nothing on any method. Seven Casino charges £5 on withdrawals under £50. Crypto withdrawals always carry a blockchain network fee, which is not the casino's charge and is typically under £1 on Litecoin or USDT. E-wallets sometimes take up to 2.5% at their end.

Are crypto withdrawals really faster?

Substantially, yes — a median of three hours against two working days for debit cards, roughly a sixteen-fold difference. Crypto also ignores weekends and bank holidays entirely and bypasses UK bank gambling blocks. The trade-off is exchange setup and price volatility, which USDT removes since it tracks the dollar.

What is the maximum I can withdraw from an online casino?

It varies by operator and it is always in the terms. Across the sites we rank, daily caps run £2,500 to £5,000 and monthly caps £20,000 to £40,000. Check whether progressive jackpot wins are exempt — where they are not, a large jackpot is paid in monthly instalments rather than as a lump sum.

Does taking a bonus slow down my withdrawal?

Yes, materially. An active bonus locks your balance until the wagering requirement is met, and any winnings above a maximum cashout limit are removed. If your priority is getting money out quickly, decline the welcome offer — your deposit then has no conditions attached to it at all.
