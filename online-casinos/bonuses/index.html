Wagering requirements: the arithmetic

The basic calculation

Wagering multiple × bonus amount = turnover you must complete before withdrawing anything.

  • £100 bonus at 35x = £3,500 of bets
  • £100 bonus at 10x = £1,000 of bets
  • £500 bonus at 40x = £20,000 of bets

That is total turnover, not total losses. A £100 balance played through at 96% RTP generates roughly £2,500 of turnover before it is exhausted, because winnings get re-staked. So a £2,500 requirement is genuinely achievable on a £100 stake; a £20,000 one is not.

The clause that doubles the work

Check whether wagering applies to the bonus or to deposit plus bonus.

  • "35x bonus" on a £100 bonus = £3,500
  • "35x deposit + bonus" on a £100 deposit matched to £100 = £7,000

Same stated multiple, double the work. Smash's 10x is on deposit plus bonus and it is still the best terms in the market, which tells you how far ahead of the field that number is.

What counts as a fair multiple

The five terms that actually cost you money

1. Maximum bet while wagering

Usually £5 per spin or 10% of the bonus, whichever is lower. Exceed it once — including accidentally, including on a single autoplay spin — and most operators void the bonus and every penny of winnings derived from it. This is the number one reason a payout is refused across this entire industry. Set your stake below the limit at the start of the session and never touch it again.

2. Maximum cashout

A cap on what you can withdraw from bonus winnings, commonly expressed as a multiple of the bonus (10x is typical) or a flat figure. Win £3,000 from a £50 bonus with a 10x cap and you withdraw £500; the rest is removed. It is always disclosed and it is the term people are angriest about discovering late. Rivo, Aphrodite and Wildzy all apply one; Smash, Kingdom, Gambiva and Seven do not state one on the welcome offer.

3. Game weighting

Casinos weight games this way because blackjack's 0.5% house edge would make bonus clearing trivially profitable otherwise. It is not a trick, but it is fatal if you assumed a "casino bonus" could be used on casino tables. Play bonus money on slots. See the live casinos page for more on this.

4. Expiry window

7 to 30 days from claiming, and the clock does not stop. A £9,500 bonus with a 14-day window is not a £9,500 bonus — it is however much you can realistically stake in fourteen days. Kingdom's 14-day window is the tightest on this page; Smash and Rivo give 30 days.

5. Excluded games

High-RTP low-volatility slots are frequently barred while a bonus is active — precisely because they are the efficient way to clear one. Blood Suckers, 1429 Uncharted Seas and Jokerizer appear on exclusion lists constantly. Check the list before you start, because playing an excluded title can void the bonus outright rather than merely failing to contribute.

Types of casino bonus explained

Welcome / matched deposit bonus

The standard: the casino matches a percentage of your deposit with bonus funds. Most valuable in absolute terms, most conditional. Everything above applies.

No deposit bonus

A small amount of bonus money or free spins for registering, with no deposit. Always carries high wagering (40x–60x) and a low cashout cap. Its real value is not the money — it is a free audition of the site's lobby, support and verification process. See no deposit casinos.

Free spins

Spins on a nominated slot at a fixed stake, usually 10p or 20p. Check three things: the spin value, the wagering on winnings, and the cashout cap. 100 spins at 10p with 40x wagering and a £50 cap is worth around £5 in expectation, not £10. Covered on free spins casinos.

Cashback

A percentage of net losses returned, weekly or monthly. Underrated. Rivo's 25% VIP cashback returns value on every session rather than once at signup, and over a year of regular play it out-earns most welcome bonuses. Check whether it is paid as cash (excellent) or as bonus funds with wagering (much less so).

Reload bonus

A smaller match on subsequent deposits, often on a fixed weekday. Kingdom's Royal Monday, Kingly Wednesday and Regal Friday calendar is the most developed on this page and is worth more to a weekly player than the welcome package.

Wager-free bonus

Rare, and the gold standard: winnings are withdrawable immediately. Where you see one, take it — even if the headline is small.

VIP and loyalty schemes

Points on turnover, converted to bonus funds or cashback. Meaningful only at high volume. Read the conversion rate: 1,000 points for £1 on a 1-point-per-£10-staked basis means £10,000 of turnover for £1. That is 0.01% back, and it is common.

When a bonus is worth taking — and when it is not

Take the bonus if

  • Wagering is 25x or below on the bonus alone
  • You play slots, at modest stakes, over several sessions
  • The expiry window matches how often you actually play
  • There is no cashout cap, or the cap is well above what you would realistically win
  • Your normal stake is comfortably under the max-bet limit

Decline the bonus if

  • Wagering is above 40x, or above 35x on deposit plus bonus
  • You mainly play live dealer, blackjack or roulette
  • You want to deposit, play briefly and withdraw — a bonus locks the whole balance
  • The cashout cap is low relative to the bonus
  • You are depositing an amount you would be uncomfortable playing through several times

How to clear a bonus efficiently

  1. Check the excluded games list first. Before your first spin. Two minutes.
  2. Set your stake below the max-bet limit and leave it. If the limit is £5, stake £2. The margin protects you from a mis-tap.
  3. Play high-RTP, low-volatility slots that contribute 100%. This maximises expected return and minimises the risk of busting before you finish. The high payout casinos page has the shortlist.
  4. Track your progress. Most cashiers show remaining wagering. Check it rather than guessing.
  5. Do not switch to live dealer "for a break". At 10% weighting you are barely progressing, and at 0% you are not progressing at all.
  6. Finish inside the window or forfeit deliberately. If you are not going to make it, forfeiting the bonus early releases your remaining real-money balance for withdrawal. Better than losing it grinding toward a target you will not reach.

Why bonuses get voided

In descending order of frequency:

  1. Exceeding the maximum bet while wagering. By some distance the most common. Often a single spin.
  2. Playing an excluded game. Bonus buys are the usual culprit.
  3. Multiple accounts. One bonus per person, household, IP address and device. Enforced, and detected.
  4. Irregular play patterns. Low-risk covering strategies on roulette — betting red and black simultaneously to generate turnover without risk — are explicitly prohibited everywhere.
  5. Withdrawing before wagering completes. At most sites this forfeits the bonus and its winnings automatically. The cashier usually warns you; read the warning.
  6. Details not matching at verification. Name, address or date of birth mismatch. Use your real details from the start.

Casino bonus FAQs

What is the best casino bonus in the UK right now?

Smash Casino's, because of its 10x wagering requirement where almost every competitor asks 35x to 40x. On a £100 deposit its 600% package demands £2,500 of turnover, against roughly £24,000 for the same headline at a 40x site. Rivo matches the 10x on a larger 1000% ladder, and Aphrodite's 200% at 25x is the most sensible option for a £20 bankroll.

What does 35x wagering actually mean?

It means 35 times the bonus amount must be staked before you can withdraw. A £100 bonus at 35x requires £3,500 of total bets. Crucially, check whether the multiple applies to the bonus alone or to deposit plus bonus — the latter doubles the work for the same stated figure. Turnover is not the same as losses, because winnings get re-staked as you go.

Is a bigger casino bonus always better?

No, and usually the opposite. The largest headline offers carry the harshest terms, because the size is what draws you in and the terms are what make it profitable. A £500 bonus at 10x with no cashout cap is worth far more in practice than a £10,000 bonus at 45x with a £500 cap. Judge an offer on turnover required and cashout cap, not on the percentage.

Why was my casino bonus taken away?

Most often because you exceeded the maximum bet while wagering — typically £5 per spin — even once. Other common causes are playing an excluded game such as a bonus-buy feature, holding multiple accounts, using low-risk covering strategies on roulette, or requesting a withdrawal before wagering completed. All of these are in the terms and all are enforced automatically.

Can I withdraw a casino bonus straight away?

Not unless it is a wager-free bonus, which is rare. Standard bonus funds are locked until the wagering requirement is met, and requesting a withdrawal beforehand usually forfeits both the bonus and any winnings from it. If you want the freedom to withdraw whenever you like, decline the welcome offer — every site here allows it, and your deposit then carries no conditions at all.

Can I use a casino bonus on blackjack or roulette?

Technically yes, effectively no. Table games contribute 5–10% toward wagering and are sometimes excluded entirely, so a £3,500 requirement becomes £35,000 or £70,000 of play. Casinos weight games this way because blackjack's 0.5% house edge would otherwise make bonus clearing trivially profitable. Clear bonuses on slots and play table games with unrestricted money.

What is a maximum cashout limit?

A cap on how much of your bonus winnings you may withdraw, usually stated as a multiple of the bonus — 10x is typical — or as a flat amount. Win £3,000 from a £50 bonus with a 10x cap and you can withdraw £500, with the remainder removed. It is always disclosed in the terms and it is the clause players most often discover too late.

Are no wagering casino bonuses real?

Yes, though they are uncommon and almost always smaller than wagered offers. Where you find one, winnings are withdrawable immediately with no turnover condition. A genuine wager-free £50 is worth more than a £500 bonus at 40x. Read the rest of the terms even so — operators usually place the constraint elsewhere, typically as a maximum bet rule or a game restriction.

How long do I have to use a casino bonus?

Between 7 and 30 days at the sites we rank, and the clock runs continuously from when you claim. Kingdom's 14-day window is the tightest here; Smash and Rivo allow 30 days. A large bonus on a short window is not really a large bonus — it is capped by how much you can realistically stake in the time available.
