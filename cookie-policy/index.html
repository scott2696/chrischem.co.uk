Cookie Policy

By Priya Raval, Editor, Regulation & Bonuses Updated 02/09/2026

Last updated 2 September 2026. This page lists every cookie this website may set, what each does, how long it lasts, and how to control them. Non-essential cookies are set only with your consent.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by a website. Cookies let a site remember things between pages and visits — your preferences, for example, or the fact that you arrived from a particular link. Similar technologies including local storage and pixels work comparably, and this policy covers those too.

Our approach

We use as few cookies as we can. Strictly necessary cookies are set automatically because the site cannot function without them; UK law permits this without consent. Analytics and marketing cookies are set only if you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can change your mind at any time.

We do not use advertising networks, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not permit third parties to track you across unrelated websites from this one.

Cookies we may set

We do not set advertising, retargeting, social media or profiling cookies.

Third-party cookies on operator sites

When you click through to a gambling operator, that operator's own cookies and tracking apply from the moment you land on its site. Those are outside our control and are governed by that operator's cookie and privacy policies, which are typically far more extensive than ours. Read them before registering.

Managing your consent

You can withdraw or change consent at any time by:

  • Reopening the cookie preferences banner and changing your selection
  • Clearing cookies for chrischem.co.uk in your browser, which resets your choice and re-presents the banner
  • Blocking cookies entirely in your browser settings

Rejecting analytics and affiliate cookies does not restrict any content on this site. Every page remains fully readable. The only practical effect is that we lose visibility of which pages are useful, and we may not be credited for a referral.

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers also offer a private or incognito mode, which discards cookies when the window closes. Note that blocking all cookies will affect many websites, not only this one.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We honour the Global Privacy Control signal where your browser sends one, treating it as a rejection of optional cookies. Browser "Do Not Track" headers are not a recognised standard and are not relied upon, so use the cookie banner or GPC to record a preference.

Legal basis

Cookies are regulated in the UK by the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) together with the UK GDPR. Strictly necessary cookies are permitted without consent. All others require your prior, informed, freely given consent, which we obtain through the cookie banner and which you may withdraw at any time.

Changes

We update this policy when our cookie use changes. The version on this page is the one in force, with the date of last update shown at the top.

Contact

Questions about cookies or data: privacy@chrischem.co.uk. See also our privacy policy and terms and conditions, or the contact us page.
